# getBusinessAccountStarBalance

Source: https://core.telegram.org/bots/api#getbusinessaccountstarbalance

[Documentation index](../llms.txt)

Returns the amount of Telegram Stars owned by a managed business account. Requires the *can\_view\_gifts\_and\_stars* business bot right. Returns [StarAmount](staramount.md) on success.

| Parameter | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| business\_connection\_id | String | Yes | Unique identifier of the business connection |
